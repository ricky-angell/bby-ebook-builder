A free Built By You Academy tool. Plan with your own ChatGPT or Claude account, write chapter by chapter, and export a professional file. Everything runs in your browser. Your book is saved on this device and never uploaded.

OPTION A

Printable PDF E-Book

For a downloadable e-book you sell or give away from your own store (Stan, Beacons, Etsy digital downloads, email lead magnets). The pages are fixed, so it looks the same everywhere and prints well.

  • US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) or A4 pages with proper margins
  • Cover page, title page, copyright page, clickable table of contents
  • Page numbers, headers and footers, images and clickable links
  • Exports a PDF file
OPTION B

Amazon Kindle E-Book

For publishing on Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP). Kindle books are reflowable: the text re-wraps to fit phones, tablets and Kindles, and readers can change the font size. So there are no fixed pages or page numbers.

  • Kindle-friendly chapters, headings and chapter breaks
  • Clickable table of contents, images with captions, links
  • Title, author, description, keywords, front and back matter
  • Exports an EPUB file for KDP, plus a Kindle cover image
Which one do I need? Selling a download from your own store → Option A (PDF). Publishing on Amazon Kindle → Option B (EPUB). Want both? Start one, then use Copy as the other type in My projects. Note: neither file is a paperback interior. A KDP paperback needs a print PDF sized to a trim size and a separate wraparound cover with a spine.

          Open the PDF

          Upload your EPUB to Amazon KDP

          1. Go to kdp.amazon.com and sign in. Click Create, then Kindle eBook.
          2. Kindle eBook Details: enter the same title, subtitle, author and description you used here, plus your keywords and categories. Check that they match the book exactly.
          3. Kindle eBook Content: under Manuscript, upload the .epub file from this tool. Under Kindle eBook Cover, choose Upload a cover you already have and upload the cover JPG from step 5.
          4. Answer the AI-generated content question honestly. KDP asks whether text, images or translations were made with AI tools.
          5. Click Launch Previewer. Check every chapter on phone, tablet and Kindle views: table of contents links, headings, pictures and links. If something is wrong, fix it here, export again and re-upload.
          6. Kindle eBook Pricing: pick territories, royalty plan and price, then publish. Amazon reviews the book before it goes live.

          Amazon changes its screens and rules from time to time. Always follow the current KDP e-book manuscript guide and cover guidelines. An EPUB is not a paperback interior. A paperback needs its own print-ready PDF and a wraparound cover with a spine (see the paperback formatting guide).

          What to do with your PDF

          1. Open the PDF and click every link in the table of contents and in your chapters.
          2. Print one or two pages to check the margins and text size.
          3. Upload it as the digital product in your Stan or Beacons store (BBY Course 04), or as an Etsy digital download.
          4. Use the cover image (step 5) as your product picture.

          This PDF is made for digital downloads. It is not a KDP paperback interior, which needs a trim size, gutter margins and a separate wraparound cover.
